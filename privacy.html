Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Kaivor is an open-source personal Android agent. It runs entirely on your device. There is no Kaivor cloud server.

What Kaivor accesses

  • Accessibility — to tap, type, and scroll in apps when you send a command from your paired Telegram chat.
  • Notifications (optional) — only if you enable notification relay in settings.
  • Internet — to reach Telegram and your chosen AI provider (Gemini, Claude, or OpenAI) using API keys you provide.

What Kaivor does not do

  • Does not sell or share your data
  • Does not upload your screen to Kaivor servers
  • Does not read SMS, contacts, or call logs at install time
  • Does not accept commands from anyone except your paired Telegram chat

Data storage

Settings, API keys, and command history are stored locally on your phone. Source code is available at github.com/aaronmphilip/Kaivor.

Contact

Issues and questions: GitHub Issues.
